Tags: [OM] = Ford 2016 Super Duty Owner's Manual; [FIELD] = technician/aftermarket consensus; [VERIFY] = confirm in the Ford WSM before use.

## Tire / wheel
- Lug nut torque: see above [OM]. Wheel pilot bore and mounting faces must be clean metal-to-metal [OM].
- Tire pressure: **use the door-jamb Tire & Loading placard** (load range E LT tires are commonly 60–80 psi; varies by tire size and axle) [OM directs to placard].
- TPMS: after a rotation, sensors relearn automatically on many Super Duty builds; otherwise use the OM "Tire Pressure Monitoring System" training procedure or a TPMS tool [VERIFY in OM text].
