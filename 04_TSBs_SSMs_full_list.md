# Ford TSBs / SSMs / Field Service Actions — 2016 F-250 Super Duty (filtered for 6.2L gas 4x4)

Source: NHTSA Manufacturer Communications flat files (TSBS_RECEIVED_2010-2024), downloaded 2026-11-20. Every Ford bulletin filed with NHTSA that names the 2016 F-250 / F-Super Duty (or a model-year range covering 2016) is included.

**How to read the flags:**
- `applies`: `yes` = bulletin text names a Super Duty/F-250 year range that includes 2016; `probable` = NHTSA indexed it against the 2016 F-250 SD but the text doesn't state the range; `unclear` = verify by VIN.
- `engine`: `gas` = mentions 6.2L/gasoline; `general` = not engine specific; diesel-only (6.7L Power Stroke) bulletins are moved to the appendix because they do NOT apply to this gas truck.
- Always confirm build-date / VIN applicability (many bulletins say 'built on or before <date>'). A Ford dealer can run the VIN through OASIS for open bulletins and FSAs.
- Full bulletin PDFs (when NHTSA has them): https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/<year>/MC-<odi_id>-9999.pdf (or -0001.pdf). Search by bulletin number at https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls (Vehicle → Manufacturer Communications).

The Powertrain Control/Emissions Diagnosis (PC/ED) manual has been revised for diagnosing electronic throttle bodies (ETBs). The ETC_ACT and ETC_DSD PIDs should not used to diagnose possible ETB concerns. The IDS has a limited refresh rate when reading these PIDs and cannot display quickly enough to validate a concern. The PCM automatically monitors these inputs more accurately and will set diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs) when appropriate. Using these PIDs for diagnostics will lead to inaccurate results and improper ETB replacements. If a concern is intermittent and no DTCs are present, refer to historical DTCs and the PC/ED, Section 3 No DTCs Present Index chart for further information.

This may be a result of using aftermarket cleaning liquids or tools for the exhaust gas recirculation (EGR) cooler or intake. Ford does not recommend the cleaning of diesel intakes or EGR coolers using any aftermarket tool that injects a cleaning foam or liquid substance into the EGR cooler or intake. Use of these cleaning tools may void the emissions warranty of the hot end aftertreatment exhaust system and the attached emissions sensors.
