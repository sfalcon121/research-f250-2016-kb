# Rear Axle (Ford/Sterling 10.50") and Driveshafts

WSM: 205-02 rear drive axle, 205-01 driveshaft. Fluid **3.5 qt 75W-140 synthetic XY-75W140-QL** plus **XL-3** for Traction-Lok [OM].

## Identification
- Ford (Sterling-design) 10.50" **full-floating** rear axle, as used on F-250/F-350 SRW Super Duty. The rear hubs protrude through the wheel center, and the axle shaft flange bolts to the hub. 37-spline shafts on 2011+ [FIELD]. Traction-Lok limited slip or optional **electronic locking differential** (dash switch) [OM].
- Ratio and LSD/locker on the door-jamb axle code and the axle tag. Typical 6.2L ratios are 3.73 or 4.30.

## Fluid change
1. Clean around the cover/fill plug. Remove the fill plug first.
2. Remove the cover (or drain plug if equipped) and drain. Clean the magnet and inspect for metal (bearing spall flakes, gear chips).
3. Clean surfaces; RTV bead (Motorcraft gasket-maker silicone per WSM; ~1/8" bead inside the bolt holes) or a gasket. Torque cover bolts in a crisscross pattern ~33 lb-ft [FIELD; VERIFY].
4. **Traction-Lok: add friction modifier XL-3 first** (4–8 oz [VERIFY]), then fill with 75W-140 to the bottom of the fill hole (~3.5 qt). Install the fill plug.
5. Locker trucks: same fluid. XL-3 isn't needed for an e-locker (open diff) [VERIFY].

## Axle shaft removal, hub bearings and hub seal (full-float)
Full-float design: the **hub rides on two tapered roller bearings on the axle housing spindle** and the axle shaft only transmits torque. The bearings are lubricated by **axle gear oil** (so a leaking hub seal puts oil on the brakes).
1. Remove the **axle shaft flange bolts** (8 typically [VERIFY]) at the hub center. Break the shaft loose (tap the flange center or use the threaded puller holes if provided) and slide the shaft out. Some oil will drain; catch it.
2. Remove the wheel, caliper and anchor bracket (anchor ~203 lb-ft at install [FIELD; VERIFY]), and rotor (hat style). Parking brake **drum-in-hat** shoes are behind it.
3. Remove the **spindle (hub) nut** retainer/lock and the nut with the correct hub nut socket (size and design per WSM [VERIFY]). Pull the hub with its bearings and the inner seal.
4. Replace the bearings and races (press/driver), and the **hub seal** (always). Clean the spindle; check the ABS tone ring.
5. **Bearing adjustment:** follow the WSM 205-02 sequence exactly. Generic Ford full-float method: seat the bearings with torque while rotating the hub, back off, then set final torque/angle to get the specified end play (typically just free with no measurable play or a small specified end play) and lock the nut [VERIFY: don't use generic numbers on a towing truck].
6. New axle flange gasket/O-ring; torque the shaft flange bolts [VERIFY]. **Refill the axle fluid and lift each side** (or wait) so oil reaches the hub bearings before driving.
7. Bleed and adjust the parking brake shoes (star adjuster through the rotor/backing plate) if disturbed.

## Pinion seal
1. Mark the pinion nut, yoke, and pinion position. **Measure turning torque (lb-in) with the wheels off and brakes free** before disassembly.
2. Remove the driveshaft, the nut (42 mm on 2011+, M30×1.0 [FIELD]), and the yoke (puller). Replace the seal.
3. Reinstall the yoke and tighten the nut **just to the previous turning torque plus 5 lb-in** or back to the original marks. **Don't over-tighten a crush sleeve.** If over-tightened, the crush sleeve must be replaced (diff teardown).

## Gear set / carrier bearings
- Crush-sleeve pinion preload (~16–29 lb-in new bearings, without the ring gear [FIELD; VERIFY]), shim pinion depth, carrier preload/backlash via adjusters or shims [VERIFY design], ring gear bolts ~111 lb-ft with threadlocker (new bolts) [FIELD; VERIFY]. Typical backlash 0.008–0.015" [VERIFY]. Check the pattern with marking compound.
- Traction-Lok clutch pack rebuild: chatter on turns → first change the fluid and add XL-3; rebuild the clutch packs if it persists.

## Driveshafts
- Rear: two-piece with a center support bearing on some cab/box combos (crew cab long bed) [VERIFY]; front: single shaft to the front diff.
- **TSB 22-2356:** rear driveline imbalance can **crack the transmission case or pan**. If there's vibration at speed or an ATF leak at the case, **check driveshaft balance/runout** and replace the rear driveline per TSB.
- U-joints: grease every oil change if serviceable. Replace with Spicer 1410/1480-series [VERIFY]. **Index-mark** the shaft to the yoke for phasing. Flange bolts ~83 lb-ft [FIELD; VERIFY]. Center-bearing replacement: press job, keep the slip-yoke phasing.
- Vibration diagnosis: by road speed (tires, driveshaft) vs. engine RPM (engine/converter); check pinion angle and center bearing after lifts or spring changes.
