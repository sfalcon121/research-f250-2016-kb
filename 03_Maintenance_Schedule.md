# Maintenance Schedule — 2016 F-250 6.2L Gasoline (Ford official) [OM pp. 456–464]

## Owner checks
- **Monthly:** air filter restriction gauge, tires including spare (wear and pressure), exhaust tailpipe holes/slots clear.
- **Lug nuts:** SRW trucks: retorque to 165 lb-ft after 100 mi (the manual's schedule page says 500 mi) following any wheel removal.
- **Multi-point inspection at every service:** drive belt, battery, air filter, exhaust, lamps, fluid levels (brake, coolant, ATF, power steering, washer), leaks, horn, hoses, suspension, steering linkage, tires, windshield, wipers.

## Normal schedule — 6.2L
| Interval | Service |
|---|---|
| **7,500 mi / 6 months** | Engine oil and filter (FL-820-S, 7.0 qt 5W-30); rotate tires and measure tread; inspect wheels/components; multi-point inspection. *Intelligent Oil Life Monitor may call for it earlier; never exceed 1 year / 7,500 mi.* |
| **15,000 mi / 12 months** | Inspect ATF level; brakes (pads, rotors, hoses, parking brake); cooling system strength and hoses; exhaust and heat shields; front axle and U-joints (lube if fittings); steering linkage, ball joints, suspension, tie-rod ends, driveshaft and U-joints (lube if fittings) |
| **30,000 mi** | Replace engine air filter (FA-1883); replace climate-seat filter (FS-104) |
| **97,500 mi** | **Replace spark plugs** (16 × SP-526, gap 0.041–0.047") |
| **105,000 mi** (or 6 yrs) | **Change engine coolant** (then every 3 yrs / 45,000 mi); inspect drive belt (then every 15k); **change 6R140 fluid and filter** (BC3Z-7A098-B) |
| **150,000 mi** | Replace drive belt if not done in the last 100k |

## Severe / special operating conditions (6.2L)
| Condition | Extra service |
|---|---|
| **Towing** or car-top carrier | Oil and filter every **5,000 mi / 6 mo**; inspect/lube U-joints; rotate tires every 5k; **transfer case fluid every 60k**; **spark plugs every 60k** |
| **Extended idle / low speed** (commercial) | Oil every 5,000 mi / 6 mo / **200 engine hours**; brakes every 5k; lube ball joints if fittings; **transfer case fluid every 30k**; plugs every 60k |
| **Dusty / sandy** | Air filter as required; oil every 5k; transfer case fluid every 60k; U-joints every 5k |
| **Off-road** | Inspect steering, ball joints, U-joints frequently; air filter; oil every 5k; transfer case fluid every 60k |
| **E85 exclusively** | Fill with regular unleaded once per oil change interval |

## Axle exceptions [OM]
- **Change axle fluid any time an axle is submerged in water.**
- Ford-design rear axles with synthetic fluid are "lubricated for life". With long towing in >70°F heat at WOT above 45 mph, change non-synthetic rear axle fluid every 3,000 mi, unless it is filled with 75W-140 synthetic (XY-75W140-QL) plus XL-3 friction modifier for Traction-Lok.

## Practical additions for a 100k+ mile truck [FIELD]
- **Front axle and rear axle fluid:** change at 60–100k anyway, and after deep water; inspect magnet for debris.
- **Transfer case XL-12:** 60k even under normal use (cheap insurance for the chain and bearings).
- **6R140:** many technicians do a pan drop and filter at 60k when towing (not a flush; refill MERCON LV only and set level hot at 196–215°F).
- **Brake fluid:** flush every 3 years (hygroscopic; the hydroboost/ABS HCU is expensive).
- **Vacuum hub lines and locking hubs:** inspect lines at each service; exercise 4x4 monthly.
- **Grease** front U-joints (if serviceable), steering linkage, driveshaft slip yoke and U-joints every oil change.
- **Battery terminals:** clean and protect (positive clamp corrosion SSM 47886).
- **Spark plugs:** many owners replace at 60k when towing to avoid seized or broken plugs.
